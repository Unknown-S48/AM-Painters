About us

A local painting team you can rely on

A&M Painters is a Hermanus-based painting and maintenance business serving homeowners, landlords, estate agents and businesses across the Overberg. We handle everything from interior repaints and roof coatings to kitchen cabinets, furniture restoration and water damage repairs.

We believe good painting is 80% preparation. Surfaces are cleaned, repaired and primed properly before a single coat goes on — which is why our work lasts longer and looks better for years, not months.

What we do

Painting & property maintenance services

One team for every surface — inside and out.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, cornices and trim — clean lines, even coverage and a proper prep job before we start.

Exterior Painting

Weatherproof coatings that protect your walls from coastal sun, wind and salt air.

Roof Painting

Roof coating, sealing and waterproofing to stop leaks and extend the life of your roof.

Door Painting

Interior and exterior doors, frames and gates — sanded, primed and finished properly.

Cabinet Painting

Kitchen and built-in cupboards resprayed or painted — a fraction of the cost of replacing them.

Furniture Painting

Give old tables, chairs and dressers a fresh look instead of buying new.

Water Damage Repairs

Repairing and repainting damaged walls and ceilings — sealed and treated so it doesn't come back.

Wood Varnishing

Decks, beams, doors and furniture varnished and sealed to bring out the grain and protect the wood.

High-Pressure Cleaning

Maintenance
